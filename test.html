Privacy

All photos and results are processed and stored on this device only, inside your browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to a server or to any AI service. Recognition uses deterministic computer‑vision and OCR techniques (edge/contour/circle detection, template and color matching, Tesseract OCR) — never machine learning, and it never invents characters it could not read.
